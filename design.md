## Purpose

A tool to summarise PDFs and turn them into easier to digest bits of information. It will be a webpage similar to the medium platform. New articles will appear perodically. 

## Design

- Medium like platform to read the articles
- perodical generation of articles from pdfs stored within the system using
llm and transformers
- perodical search of related materials from the internet (arxiv for example) to generate new articles
- ability to add pdfs/links on the platform to start generation
- image generation for the articles, for the look and feel, plus to make the information easier to comprehend. 

## Spefication

- use huggintree models to aid in summerisation and categorisation
- use airllm to support using bigger llm models without tanking the machine
